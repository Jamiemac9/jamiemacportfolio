    Supported housing • Audit readiness • Evidence

    Support Audit

    Property compliance comes with certificates, expiry dates and a contractor who signs. Support compliance has none of that. Support Audit checks whether a provider's support evidence would stand up if a regulator or commissioner looked at it.

    • Evidence first — every file is checked against a clear standard for what good evidence looks like
    • An auditor makes the call — a person sets every final grade
    • Three lanes — exempt, supported and transitional housing, with the pathways between them
    Visit supportaudit.co.uk

    Work game • Agent fleet • Cloudflare • Real-time

    Cogent Island

    Cogent Island is a live work game. A 3D village grows as Jamie's agent fleet does real work. A sale, an outreach send or a clean Hermes run all score points on the island. Six villagers each react to a different type of work. Points, bells, streaks and per-brand districts keep it moving. It runs on a Cloudflare Worker with a D1 ledger and a Durable Object that pushes live updates to every open device at once.

    • Visible momentum — every sale, outreach send and clean Hermes run scores points on the island in real time
    • Six agent-villagers — each reacts to a different type of work, with their own letters at each level
    • Live from the ledgers — the APX operating data syncs every 15 minutes, so pipeline movement and closed deals appear without manual entry
    • Real time, real seasons — the sky follows UK time, the trees change with the month, and every open device sees the same island

    Supported housing • Free tools • Regulation tracker

    ExemptAccommodation.com

    Supported housing rules, without the jargon. A plain-English hub on exempt, supported, transitional and emergency accommodation for support providers, landlords, managing agents and proptech teams. It explains Housing Benefit rent and service charges, licensing and regulation, and tracks what's changing.

    • Free tools — an audit readiness check (24 questions across eight areas), an exempt accommodation checker and a service charge calculator
    • Free templates — support plan, needs assessment, rent and service charge schedule, licensing readiness checklist
    • Regulation tracker — 19 guides that cite the legislation, a timeline of changes from the Renters' Rights Act to council licensing expected in 2027, and email alerts
    • Backed by an audit firm — published by SEA Audit, for when a provider wants its evidence checked properly
    Visit exemptaccommodation.com

    Questions people ask

    The short answers

    Who is Jamie Mac?

    Jamie Mac is a founder based in Solihull, West Midlands, working where property, technology and compliance meet. He has spent eight years in property and compliance across HMOs, social, supported and exempt accommodation, with people whose needs ranged from low-level support to high support, including DoLS and care leavers aged 16 to 18. He has led a team of 20+ as Director of Housing, led Birmingham City Council's exempt accommodation inspection team, audited providers against RSH and CQC standards, sourced, scaled and sold HMO portfolios, and worked in prop tech at Howsy.

    He has also spent nearly ten years in technology, from his first websites in 2017 to cyber security, full-stack development and cloud architecture. Today he helps companies and regulated industries adopt digital tools wisely and integrate the cloud. He runs Support Audit, ExemptAccommodation.com, APX Digital and Cogent Intelligence on a team of real AI agents, and he is a Level 4 personal trainer with 13 years of coaching at High Performance Fit.

    What property types and support needs has Jamie worked with?

    HMOs, social housing, supported housing, exempt accommodation, transitional housing and private lettings. Support needs ranged from low-level support to high support, including people under Deprivation of Liberty Safeguards (DoLS) and care leavers aged 16 to 18.

    What is Support Audit?

    Support Audit checks whether a supported housing provider's property and support compliance evidence would stand up to a regulator or commissioner. It audits resident support files for Registered Providers and managing agents, and an auditor sets every grade. It does not deliver or sign off the compliance work itself.

    What is ExemptAccommodation.com?

    A free, plain-English hub on exempt, supported, transitional and emergency accommodation. It explains Housing Benefit rent and service charges, licensing and regulation, and offers an audit readiness check, an exempt accommodation checker, a service charge calculator, templates and a regulation tracker.

    Does Jamie build AI agents for other businesses?

    Yes, through Cogent Intelligence. His own businesses run on a team of real agents (Hermes, Claude, Scout, Grok and Sprig), with business rules kept in the database and people approving anything that goes out.

    Where is Jamie based, and who does he work with?

    Solihull, West Midlands, working with organisations across the UK, particularly supported and exempt accommodation providers, Registered Providers, managing agents and small and medium-sized businesses.
